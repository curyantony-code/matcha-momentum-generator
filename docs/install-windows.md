# Installing Matcha Momentum Generator on Windows

This takes about two minutes. You do not need to be an administrator, and nothing else needs to be installed first.

**What you need:** Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit), about 2 GB of free space, and an internet connection the first time you open the app.

## 1. Download

Download the file called **matcha-momentum-generator-setup-1.0.0-x64.exe** (the numbers may be a little different). It usually lands in your **Downloads** folder.

## 2. Open it

Double-click the file.

### If Windows shows a blue box saying "Windows protected your PC"

Don't worry, this is normal, and it does not mean something is wrong with the file.

Windows shows this box for any program from a small or new publisher until enough people have installed it without trouble, and it starts over with every new version. So you may see it even when the box names Matcha Momentum Generator's publisher instead of "Unknown publisher". You can still install it:

1. Click the small words **More info** (in the middle of the box, under the message).
2. A new button appears at the bottom. Click **Run anyway**.

That's it. You only have to do this once.

### If your browser says "this file isn't commonly downloaded"

In Edge or Chrome, click the three dots next to the download, then **Keep**, then **Keep anyway**. Then open the file as above.

## 3. Wait a minute

A small window shows the install progress. When it finishes, Matcha Momentum Generator opens by itself and puts an icon on your desktop and in the Start menu.

## 4. First time you open it

Matcha Momentum Generator downloads its speech helper once (this is how it "listens" to your video). Keep the window open until the bar finishes. It only happens this once, and after that Matcha Momentum Generator works without internet for everything except finding images and music online.

## Where things go

* The app itself: `C:\Users\<you>\AppData\Local\Programs\reel-studio` (the folder name is lower case with a hyphen; the Start menu and desktop shortcuts are called **Matcha Momentum Generator**)
* Your videos, music and exports: **Documents > Matcha Momentum Generator** (open it any time, the folders have plain names)

## Updating to a newer version

Matcha Momentum Generator does not update itself or check for updates. New versions are posted on the
[releases page](https://github.com/curyantony-code/matcha-momentum-generator/releases) (also **Settings > Get a newer version**).
Install the new one over the old: your videos, music and settings are untouched.

## Removing it

Open **Settings > Apps > Installed apps**, find **Matcha Momentum Generator**, click the three dots, then **Uninstall**. Your **Documents > Matcha Momentum Generator** folder is left alone, so you never lose your videos by accident.

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| "Run anyway" never appears | Click **More info** first. The button only shows up after that. |
| The installer vanished after downloading | Your antivirus moved it. Open your antivirus, look for "quarantine" or "protection history", and choose **Restore**. Then try again. |
| "Not enough disk space" | Free up 2 GB (empty the Recycle Bin, delete old downloads) and run the installer again. |
| The first-time download stops | Check your internet, close Matcha Momentum Generator, and open it again. It carries on where it stopped. |
| Anything else | Restart the computer, install again. Your videos are never touched by installing. |
